- free models: honour the hyphenated `-free` id suffix when classifying a fetched model as free. OpenCode marks its free tier with `-free` (not OpenRouter's `:free`) and serves `/models` rows as bare `{ id, name }` with no pricing or `free` flag, so `isFreeModel` rejected every one of them — a free-only sync imported just `big-pickle`, dropping the other 9 free models and leaving the synced availability list too short to scope the `oc/` catalog. Gated on a documented free tier, so a `-free` id on a provider without one still stays paid.
